The reply audits a billing folder in which a Renewal class creates its mailer inline with `new SmtpMailer(...)` inside
its method.

PASS if: The audit names the inline creation of the mailer as a finding, and gives a cure such as passing the mailer
in (through the constructor or as an argument) so the class can be used and tested with another mailer.
FAIL if the reply does not meet that, or contradicts it.
