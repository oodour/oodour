The reply advises on a `Checkout` class that walks its `Inventory` collaborator through each step of reserving an
order.

PASS if: It recommends that `Checkout` send one message that states what it wants (for example
`inventory.reserveFor(order)`), with the checking and reserving steps moved behind that message in `Inventory`, and
it says that `Inventory`'s step methods that only `Checkout` used (such as `stockLevels`, `reserve` or
`markReserved`) can then become private or be dropped from its public interface.
FAIL if the reply does not meet that, or contradicts it.
