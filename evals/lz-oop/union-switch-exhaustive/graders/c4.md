The reply judges an exhaustive `switch` over a discriminated union of incoming payment events that does each kind's work, when a new kind and a second such `switch` are about to be added.

PASS if: It accepts the union itself as a reasonable shape for the parsed webhook data and does not ask for the incoming payload type to be replaced; its objection is to switching on `kind` to do the work, not to the union.
FAIL if the reply does not meet that, or contradicts it.
