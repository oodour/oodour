# Attribution

The skills, references and agents in this plugin are original writing. They teach object-oriented design as Sandi Metz
teaches it, together with Katrina Owen and TJ Stankus, and draw on the works below. The plugin quotes none of them and
cites none of them in its working text; this file is where the credit lives.

## Books

- Sandi Metz, Katrina Owen and TJ Stankus, _99 Bottles of OOP_, 2nd edition, JavaScript, and its companion code. Where
  this plugin's guidance and another work differ, this book decides.
- Sandi Metz, _Practical Object-Oriented Design: An Agile Primer Using Ruby_, 2nd edition (2018).
- Martin Fowler, _Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code_, 2nd edition (2018).
- Joshua Kerievsky, _Refactoring to Patterns_ (2004).
- Gerard Meszaros, _xUnit Test Patterns: Refactoring Test Code_ (2007).
- Gamma, Helm, Johnson and Vlissides, _Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software_ (1994).
- Rebecca Wirfs-Brock and Alan McKean, _Object Design: Roles, Responsibilities, and Collaborations_ (2002).
- Michael Feathers, _Working Effectively with Legacy Code_ (2004).
- Kent Beck, _Test-Driven Development: By Example_ (2002).
