The reply rules on whether a small template-method hierarchy should be rewritten as composition.

PASS if: It supports that with at least two properties of this hierarchy, such as: it is one level deep, the subclasses are leaves, each subclass uses everything the parent provides, or the parent supplies a harmless default where a hook is optional.
FAIL if the reply does not meet that, or contradicts it.
